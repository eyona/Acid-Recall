## The machines, kept familiar

Two TB-303s, a TR-808, a TR-909 and an eight-lane sampler share the Graphite rack, with
effects on the right and existing knobs and mixer strips in place.

- **TR patterns:** 16 steps by default, with 32/64-step phrases and left-side page selection,
  COPY, PASTE, DUP and FOLLOW. Rack size and controls stay fixed when the length changes.
  Hidden pages retain their notes, and Groove Generator follows the complete phrase.
- **TB editing:** choose note names from the right-click grid; A/S flags show Accent and
  Slide directly on the keys. Rest and octave controls remain available.
- **Musical tools:** Techno, IDM and Break generation, linked TB patterns, Undo Random
  and one arpeggiator per TB.
- **Readable controls:** compact routing labels, CON/CLA/MAR sound switches, separated
  SPACE labels and a MASTER panel with its own COMP button.

## Sampler Focus and WARP

The Focus sampler groups its waveform, playheads and LOAD/AUDITION controls, with
Pitch/Level/Reverb knobs, ONE SHOT, AUTO CUT, GATE, LOOP and REVERSE. Eight lanes support
sequences up to 64 bars. Solo/Mute and right-click sample unloading remain within reach.

WARP fits samples to tempo while retaining pitch. The numbered beat grid supports local
stretch anchors, snapping and zoom. Optional BPM/transient analysis offers half/double BPM
controls and suggested anchors. Sampler/WARP undo and redo cover sample loading/unloading.

GATER and SIDECHAIN have independent source and instrument choices. A sampler lane can
follow GLOBAL sidechain settings or select its own TR-808/TR-909 trigger voice. Explicit
lane routing works with global TB sidechain bypassed.

Stereo convolution reverb supports IR import. Delay has a STEPS knob and STRAIGHT/TRIPLET
timing on the rack. The sampler retains its MIX strip alongside the four original machines.

## Controllers, sync and audio

- **MIDI Learn:** right-click to learn or remove an assignment, with an optional MAX from
  0 to 127 for continuous controls. Multiple inputs, soft takeover and portable mappings
  are supported. Machine and effect switches use absolute 0/127 states.
- **MIDI Clock:** MASTER/OUT and SLAVE/IN, 24 PPQN, Start/Continue/Stop and Song Position Pointer.
  New OUT configurations start at 30 ms delay; IN has a signed phase offset and stable
  nominal BPM display. Stop/Space rewinds to measure 1.
- **Metering and balance:** every-sample peak measurement, orange from −6 dBFS, red at
  0 dBFS, peak hold and CLIP latch. Master L/R values show dBFS before output clipping.
  Both TR trims default to 0 dB and act after insert effects.
- **Audio preferences:** choose ASIO or WASAPI, output device, stereo pair, sample rate
  and buffer size. Preferences are saved locally. ASIO requires the interface's installed
  64-bit Windows driver; the SDK is not needed to run the portable application.

## Portable projects

New Save As operations default to **.recall**. Projects embed the song, samples, IR and
Studio settings, including extended drum patterns. Existing **.orb338** projects still open;
Save keeps their filename and extension. The binary container format has not changed.
The legacy .rbs format retains the native first sixteen drum steps.

## Release scope

The official 1.0 package is **Windows x64 portable**. macOS and Linux remain source-build
targets; this release does not include Android or iPad apps. Automated checks and user
testing do not cover every audio interface, MIDI driver, controller or receiving machine.
Adjust buffer sizes and clock offsets for your own equipment.
